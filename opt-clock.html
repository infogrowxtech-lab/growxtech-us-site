How the OPT unemployment clock actually works

If you're on post-completion F-1 OPT, USCIS allows a maximum of 90 cumulative days of unemployment during your 12-month OPT period. If you later get the 24-month STEM OPT extension, you get an additional 60 days, for a combined cumulative maximum of 150 days across the full OPT + STEM OPT period. Cross that limit and you're considered out of F-1 status, which can affect a future H-1B change of status and other visa applications.

The clock isn't measured from when you started searching, it runs from your official OPT start date on your I-20, and it counts every day you weren't in qualifying paid work in your field, not just full unemployment stretches. This tool adds those periods up for you.

This calculator is for planning purposes only. It is not legal or immigration advice, and Growx Tech IT is not a law firm or your school's Designated School Official (DSO). If you're within 15-20 days of your limit, talk to your DSO immediately, they're the only ones who can officially confirm your status and options.

Frequently asked questions

How many unemployment days am I allowed on OPT?
F-1 students on standard post-completion OPT are allowed a maximum of 90 days of unemployment during their 12-month OPT period. Exceed that and you're considered out of status.
How many unemployment days am I allowed on STEM OPT?
The 24-month STEM OPT extension adds 60 more allowable days, for a combined cumulative limit of 150 days across your initial OPT plus the STEM extension, not 150 days on top of a fresh 90.
When does the OPT clock start?
On your OPT start date listed on your I-20, not your graduation date and not the date you started job hunting.
What happens if I go over the limit?
You're considered to have violated F-1 status, which can complicate a future H-1B change of status or other immigration steps. Talk to your school's DSO right away if you're approaching the limit.
Does the H-1B cap-gap extension add more unemployment days?
No. Cap-gap extends your work authorization between your STEM OPT end date and H-1B start date if your petition was timely filed as a change of status, but it's separate from, and doesn't add to, your 90/150-day unemployment allowance.
