What the local tech market looks like right now

DFW is a telecom/enterprise-IT and banking-tech corridor, anchored by AT&T’s telecom operations and Texas Instruments’ semiconductor engineering, with a fast-growing fintech/financial-services tech cluster concentrated in Plano, nicknamed "Y’all Street."

Worth knowing

AT&T recently announced a new, multibillion-dollar global headquarters campus in Plano, consolidating its DFW operations, while Plano's financial-services sector has grown to well over a hundred companies and tens of thousands of jobs.

DFW pairs a dense concentration of Fortune 500 tech/telecom headquarters with no state income tax and a lower cost of living than coastal tech hubs.
