9 metros · Fully remote process

Placement strength across 9 major U.S. tech markets.

Growx places IT and tech candidates nationwide, with the deepest track record in these nine metros. The process is the same everywhere: consultation, resume and LinkedIn, recruiter-led marketing, training and interview prep, all remote, no local office required.

Washington

Seattle

IT professionals target Seattle for direct access to two of the world's largest cloud/enterprise employers and the ecosystem built around them.

 California

Silicon Valley / San Francisco Bay Area

IT professionals target the Bay Area for the highest concentration of AI/software company headquarters and the highest average tech compensation in the U.S.

 Texas

Austin

IT professionals target Austin for a lower cost of living and no state income tax, combined with a still-substantial base of hardware, semiconductor and enterprise-tech employers.

 New York

New York City

IT professionals target NYC for the sheer scale and diversification of tech roles: Wall Street, media, retail and pure tech all compete for the same talent pool.

 Illinois

Chicago

IT professionals target Chicago for lower cost of living than the coasts combined with deep, durable demand from finance, healthcare and logistics employers.

 Massachusetts

Boston

IT professionals target Boston for its unmatched density of research institutions feeding a tight loop between software, biotech and well-funded startups.

 Texas

Dallas–Fort Worth

DFW pairs a dense concentration of Fortune 500 tech/telecom headquarters with no state income tax and a lower cost of living than coastal tech hubs.

 Georgia

Atlanta

IT professionals target Atlanta for its unmatched depth in payments/fintech engineering roles combined with a lower cost of living than NYC or SF for comparable salaries.

 North Carolina

Raleigh-Durham

The Triangle offers deep specialization in enterprise open-source software and life-sciences/biotech IT within a lower-cost, university-anchored talent market.

Don't see your city? We still place you.

Growx works with candidates nationwide, remotely. These nine metros just reflect where placement activity is strongest right now.
