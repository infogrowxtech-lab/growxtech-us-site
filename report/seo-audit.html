02 Competitor dossiers

One real strength worth studying, and the specific thing to take from each, not generic advice.

CareerUS Solution

careerussolution.com

13 separate service/solution URLs instead of one services page: each targets a different long-tail search.

Steal: split your services page into individual URLs per offer (resume, LinkedIn, training, interview prep, mentorship).

Codex Tech IT

codextech-it.com

3-tier pricing page + Canada landing page + $500 referral page. But the exact same page title on every page: a real penalty, not a strength.

Steal: a country/region-specific landing page, the way they did for Canada.

InfiJobs

infijobs.pro

Named founder/team bios with LinkedIn links, E-Verify/DHS-SSA trust badges, testimonials that name JPMorgan, Google, Amazon as placement outcomes.

Steal: named-employer proof in testimonials reads far more credible than a star rating.

Helix Tech Inc

helixtechinc.com

73 pages: a landing page per course, per service, per city (New York, LA, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix...) plus free tools (resume builder, cover letter examples).

Steal: free resume/interview-prep tools, cheap to build, strong AEO bait.

Lone Wolf Recruitment

lonewolfrecruitment.com

robots.txt explicitly names GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot as welcome: a deliberate AEO play. 31 posts on a city × niche template ("HR Consultancy in Chicago for Indian Students").

Steal: already matched, we shipped the same AI-crawler allow list today (see below).

Wolf Technologies

wolftechnologiesllc.com

15 blog posts, all titled as questions ("Why Communication Skills Matter More Than Coding in U.S. Tech Jobs?"), exactly the shape AI answer engines lift.

Steal: question-titled articles. Don't copy their "Application Guaranteed" package naming: that's a promise Charlie is deliberately built never to make.

JHEx

jhex.info

Only 9 pages and single-word titles ("Jhex," "Plans"), genuinely weak. Its one asset: Google Reviews embedded directly on the pricing page.

Steal: embedding real reviews next to the price people are about to pay.

Bytewave Technology

bytewavetechnology.com

Built a full blog infrastructure (5 categories), then never published to it. A cautionary example, not a model.

Steal: nothing structural, but it's proof a half-started blog is worse than no blog. If we build one, we finish it.
