IT career services

Everything between "I want a U.S. job" and the offer letter.

Most candidates lose months bouncing between a resume writer, a training institute, and a job board that never replies. Growx runs all six stages under one roof, with one team that knows your file.

Stage 01 · 1–2 days

Free Career Consultation

Before anyone touches your resume, an advisor works out what you should actually be applying for.

A

Goal alignment

What role, what salary band, what timeline, written down so every later stage points at the same target.

B

Technical assessment

An honest read on where your skills sit against current U.S. job descriptions, and what's missing.

C

Career plan

A written roadmap: which stages you need, in what order, and roughly how long each will take.

Stage 02 · 7–10 days

Resume Writing & LinkedIn Optimization

Most resumes are rejected by software before a human sees them. We rebuild yours to pass applicant tracking systems and still read well to a hiring manager.

A

ATS-friendly rebuild

Clean structure, real keywords from the job descriptions you're targeting, and verified experience written in the format U.S. recruiters expect.

B

LinkedIn makeover

Headline, About, and experience rewritten so recruiters searching your skills actually find you, plus visibility settings most candidates get wrong.

C

Personal branding kit

A consistent profile across the platforms recruiters check, so your story matches everywhere they look.

Stage 03 · Ongoing

Recruiter-Led Profile Marketing

This is the difference between applying and being marketed. A dedicated recruiter puts your profile in front of people who hire.

A

Company targeting

A shortlist of employers and staffing partners that actually hire your profile, instead of spraying applications everywhere.

B

Active promotion

Your recruiter submits and follows up, up to 100 applications a day on the higher tiers, with daily reports so you see exactly what went out.

C

Network outreach

Direct outreach through hiring networks and premium job portals you can't reach from a free account.

Stage 04 · 20 days

Technical Training for IT Roles

Interviews expose skill gaps fast. Twenty days of structured, hands-on work closes them before you're in the room.

A

Core skill workshops

Live sessions on the fundamentals your target role tests, not recorded videos you'll never finish.

B

Hands-on projects

Real project work you can talk about in an interview and point to on your resume.

C

Technical assessments

Regular evaluation with written reports, so you know you're ready rather than hoping you are.

Stage 05 · Ongoing

Interview Preparation & Mock Interviews

Preparation is the stage candidates skip and then regret. We run it until you're steady.

A

Recorded mock interviews

Full-length practice interviews, recorded so you can watch yourself and fix the specific things that cost you offers.

B

Interview coordination

Scheduling handled for you across time zones, so nothing gets missed while you're at your current job.

C

Behavioral coaching

The soft-skill half most technical candidates lose on, communication, framing your story, salary conversations.

Stage 06 · Ongoing

Career Mentorship After Placement

The relationship doesn't end at the offer letter, the first ninety days in a new role decide the next five years.

A

Dedicated mentorship

Someone to call when you're navigating a new team, a new manager, or a new country.

B

Career tracking

Regular check-ins on where you're heading next, not just where you landed.

C

Continuous support

Ongoing access to the team for the next move, whenever it comes.
